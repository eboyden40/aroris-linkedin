01

The Message

Every team member should be able to speak to these three things clearly and consistently. This is the foundation everything else builds on.

What we do

Revenue Governance for Healthcare Providers.

Aroris is how modern practices grow and protect the revenue they have already earned, and the revenue they are still owed.

The problem we solve

Most practices do not know what their contracts say, what they are owed, or what their peers are paid. Payers do. That information gap is where 5 to 14% of revenue disappears every month, without anyone catching it.

What makes us different

Aroris governs revenue end to end. Contract intelligence, expert negotiation, and live claims monitoring, all running on a single platform. We are not billing software. We do not start at the claim. We start with the contract.

05

Response Guide

Use these when someone comments on a post or reaches out via DM. They are starting points. Personalize the language but stay on message. Reply within 24 hours whenever possible.

If they say...You say...
"What does Aroris do?" We govern revenue for healthcare practices: contract optimization, payer negotiation, and live claims monitoring, all on one platform. Happy to walk you through it if you are curious.
"How are you different from RCM?" We work upstream from billing. We start with what your contracts say, what payers owe you based on those contracts, and what leverage you have going into the next negotiation. Most RCM stops at the claim. We start with the contract.
"How does pricing work?" We align our compensation with your outcomes, so we are on the same side as the practice. Best to walk through it for your specific situation: aroris.com or DM me directly.
"Is this only for large practices?" Not at all. Code-level benchmarking surfaces more opportunity for mid-size practices who have not had access to this kind of data before. We work across all sizes and all 50 states.
"What specialties do you cover?" All of them. We work with primary care, orthopedics, behavioral health, radiology, pain management, surgery centers, and more. The governance model is the same; the data and rate-case logic is tailored to the specialty.
"I would love to learn more" Head to aroris.com to see your number in 60 seconds, or DM me and I will set up a quick call.
Warm outreach DM template

Hey [Name], I wanted to reach out because [specific reason: their specialty, role, or something you know about their practice]. Aroris helps healthcare practices govern their revenue. Thought it might be worth 10 minutes. aroris.com if you want to take a look, or happy to walk you through it directly.

06

Profile Update Checklist

Run through this list when you update your profile. Everything here should be in place before you post anything on behalf of Aroris.

Banner and visuals

  • Download your chosen banner from Section 02 and upload to LinkedIn (Edit background photo)
  • Profile photo is a clear, professional headshot. No busy backgrounds

Headline and About

  • Headline updated with your role-specific copy from Section 03
  • About section updated with boilerplate from Section 04, with your personal opening line
  • Current role shows "Aroris" with the correct title
  • Website field is set to aroris.com

Company alignment

  • Following the Aroris company page
  • Your current role is linked to the Aroris company page (not just typed in)
  • Contact info is current and reachable

Before posting

  • Stats used are from the approved list in Section 07. Never estimate or round differently
  • No payer names, client practice names, or competitor names in public posts
  • Any questions on copy or messaging go to the brand custodian before posting

Say these

  • Revenue Governance: capital R, capital G
  • Aroris360: one word, capital A
  • Govern, operate, monitor, negotiate, protect
  • Rate cases, rate lift, reimbursement rates
  • Practices: not clients or customers
  • Ungoverned revenue: it is an Aroris term

Never say these

  • Specific revenue guarantees for individual practices
  • Payer names in public posts
  • Competitor company names
  • Client practice names without approval
  • AI-powered, game-changing, disruptive, best-in-class
  • Stats not on this list without checking first
